It ships as a SEPARATE signal next to the rule-based verdict, not a
replacement for it.

CAVEAT ON THE REJECT-INFERENCE PART, from reject_inference_truth_report.txt:
scored against this synthetic dataset's (otherwise unobservable) true
outcome for declined applicants, parceling scored WORSE than doing no
reject inference at all (AUC -0.0011, KS -0.0073 vs. accepts-only). A real
deployment cannot run that check -- it needs a randomised approval slice --
so this model is shown as what reject inference produces, not endorsed as
an improvement over the alternative.
